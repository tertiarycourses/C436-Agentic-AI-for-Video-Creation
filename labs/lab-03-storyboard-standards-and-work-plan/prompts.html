Lab 03 — Storyboard Artifact, Editing Standards and Work Plan

Claude Desktop — the storyboard skill and artifacts · about 60 minutes

Turn the approved script into the documents a production team — human or AI — works from, and approve the storyboard before any credit is spent.

Steps

  1. Lock the characters — Prompt A: the asset bible — five fixed traits each for Rachel, Ah Ma and the blue tin, plus a reference prompt for each.
  2. Storyboard as an artifact — Prompt B calls the storyboard skill. Claude opens a shot-card storyboard marked DRAFT beside the chat.
  3. Pick the camera language — In the Prompt Builder (Stage Storyboard), tick shot sizes, angles and camera moves for each shot and paste them in as changes.
  4. Review, then approve — Prompt C requests changes. Only when every shot is right, reply APPROVED; the banner turns green.
  5. Write the standards — Prompt D: the studio's editing standards and guidelines, legal and platform rules included.
  6. Plan the post-production — Prompt E gives a work plan; save it as work-plan.csv and set real dates.
  7. Peer review — Swap storyboards with a partner. Each names one continuity risk in the other's board.

PROMPT A — character and asset bible
Create the asset bible for Kopi & Coins.

For Rachel Tan (29), Ah Ma (74) and the blue butter-cookie
tin, give: role in the story, five visible traits that must
never change (face, hair, clothing, accessories, colours), how
they move and speak, and one reference-image prompt each
(front view, neutral light, plain background, 9:16).

Rachel: shoulder-length black hair, round tortoiseshell
glasses, mustard-yellow cardigan over a white T-shirt.
Ah Ma: short silver permed hair, jade bangle, floral blouse.
Save it to project knowledge as 02-storyboard__asset-bible.
PROMPT B — storyboard artifact (storyboard skill)
Use the storyboard skill on the approved Episode 1 script.

Make the storyboard as an HTML artifact: six to eight shot cards
for TikTok, 9:16, 60 seconds. Each card: shot ID (S01-SH01),
time, shot size, camera angle, camera move (a Higgsfield preset
name where one fits), the one action, characters, dialogue or
VO, on-screen text, sound, continuity line and asset references.

Faces in the upper third; text clear of the bottom 20% of the
frame (35% if you also post to Reels). Respect the 180-degree rule in the video-call scene.
Mark it DRAFT and stop for my review.
PROMPT C — request changes, then approve
Changes to the storyboard:
- S01-SH01: start on the $1,400 repair quote, ECU, camera
  [CAMERA_MOVE, e.g. Dolly In].
- S03-SH01: high angle on the tin, camera Static.
- Payoff shot: mirror the framing of the hook.
Keep every other shot unchanged. Show me the new version.

(When every shot is right, reply only: APPROVED)
PROMPT D — editing standards and guidelines
Write the editing standards: the rules every Kopi & Coins
episode must meet before it is published.

Sections:
1. Brand: colours, fonts, logo position, end card.
2. Story: hook in the first 3 seconds, one message, pacing.
3. Captions: always on, accuracy, line length, safe area.
4. Audio: dialogue clarity, music under voice, loudness target.
5. Legal: music and image rights, no personal data, money
   messaging (general education only, no promised returns,
   disclaimer), labelling AI-generated content.
6. Platform: 9:16, 1080x1920, frame rate, duration limits,
   TikTok and YouTube AI-content labels.

Make every rule checkable, with a number or a yes/no test.
PROMPT E — post-production work plan
Create a post-production work plan for Episode 1 as CSV with
columns: stage, task, owner, start day, duration (hours),
depends on, review gate, done when.

Stages in order: reference stills in Higgsfield, low-res draft
shots, upgrade approved shots to 1080p, assembly in LahLah
Video, story review, fine cut, sound mix, captions, technical
QC, compliance review, approval, export, publish.

We have 10 working days. Put a human approval gate before
publish, and allow one day for regenerating failed shots.

  • The storyboard banner reads APPROVED, and the approved file is saved as 02-storyboard__ep01-approved.
  • The standards cover brand, story, captions, audio, legal and platform — each rule checkable.
  • work-plan.csv lists every stage with an owner, a duration and a review gate, and fits 10 working days.
