# Video Prompts for Lab 01

## Main Hermes prompt

```text
Help me create a 15-second vertical video for Harbour Bean. Audience: busy office workers. Message: check your coffee grind. Show three five-second shots: an attention-grabbing close-up, a clear comparison, and a closing save-this-guide message. Warm morning light, calm practical tone, slow camera movement, readable captions, no invented claims. First show me the shot list; after I approve it, create the video and ask me what should improve.
```

## Simple prompt pattern

`Create a [length] [format] video for [audience] about [message]. Show [shot 1], [shot 2] and [shot 3]. Use [lighting], [camera movement] and [tone]. Include [captions or voice]. End with [call to action]. First show me the shot list.`

## Weak to strong

Weak: `Make a cool coffee video.`

Stronger: `Create a 15-second vertical coffee tip for busy office workers. Begin with an extreme close-up of uneven coffee grounds, cut to a clear side-by-side comparison, and finish on a warm cup beside the words Save this guide. Use soft morning light, slow push-in movement and a calm practical tone.`


## Review prompt

```text
Review the result as a first-time viewer. Tell me what is clear, what is confusing, where attention drops, whether the visual style stays consistent, and the three changes with the greatest impact. Refer to exact times or shot numbers.
```

## Revision prompt

```text
Create a new version that changes only the approved review points. Keep the story message, approved references and successful shots unchanged. Show me what changed before replacing the current draft.
```
