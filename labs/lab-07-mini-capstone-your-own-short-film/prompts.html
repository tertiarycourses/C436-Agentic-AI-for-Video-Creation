Lab 07 — Mini Capstone: Your Own Short Film, Idea to Upload

Claude Desktop + Higgsfield + LahLah Video — all skills, platform-publish · about 70 minutes

Prove you can run the whole pipeline on a story of your own: approve the board, generate on a budget, cut, check, publish privately with the AI label on, and plan the adoption with your own numbers.

Steps

  1. Pitch your film — Any story you like, 20-45 seconds, 3-5 shots, fictional people only. Fill short-film-brief-template.pdf (see the Ah Ma sample).
  2. New project — Create "Capstone – [FILM TITLE]" from project-capstone-setup.pdf; add your brief and your standards.
  3. Idea to approved board — Prompt A chains storytelling and storyboard: three loglines, then the brief, asset bible and board. Approve it.
  4. Cast, then shoot — Prompt B: references saved as Elements, low-res drafts with dialogue in quotes, upgrade the keepers, hard budget.
  5. Cut and export — Open lahlah-edit.json in LahLah Video; captions, music under the voices, end card, AI label; export EP02_v1.mp4.
  6. Check, publish privately, plan — Run kopi-compliance-review, then Prompt C (platform-publish, Private or draft) and Prompt D (adoption plan).

Prompts

Change only the [HIGHLIGHTED] words, then Copy and paste into the Claude project chat.

PROMPT A — your idea to an approved storyboard
Use the storytelling skill, then the storyboard skill.

My short film: [ONE-LINE IDEA]. Genre [GENRE], for
[PLATFORM], 9:16, [LENGTH: 20-45] seconds, [3-5] shots,
fictional characters only: [WHO].

Give me three loglines and stop. After I pick one, write the
brief, the asset bible (characters, location, props) and the
storyboard artifact marked DRAFT, then stop for my approval.
PROMPT B — cast, then shoot on a hard budget
Use the video-script skill on the approved storyboard, then
the higgsfield-video skill.

1. References first: one 9:16 still per character, location
   and key prop; save the ones I approve as Elements.
2. Draft every shot at 480p on Seedance 2.0 Mini, sound on;
   put each spoken line in quotes with the speaker named.
3. Upgrade to 1080p only the drafts I approve.

Hard limit: [BUDGET] credits in total; ask before each batch.
Higgsfield project: "Capstone – [FILM TITLE]". Finish with the
status table, download links, lahlah-edit.json (fps = the
clips' frame rate) and captions.srt.
PROMPT C — publish privately (platform-publish skill)
Use the platform-publish skill for EP02_v1 ("[FILM TITLE]")
on [PLATFORM].

Give me: export settings, a title under 60 characters, a
description with the AI disclosure, five hashtags, a
thumbnail prompt, and the exact clicks to switch on the
AI-content label. The upload stays Private (YouTube) or a
draft (TikTok). Do not publish anything yourself.
PROMPT D — adoption plan
Write an adoption plan for running Kopi & Coins on Claude,
Higgsfield and LahLah Video.

Use my numbers: Episode 1 took [HOURS] hours and [CREDITS]
credits; my capstone film took [HOURS] hours and [CREDITS]
credits; low-res drafting saved about [SAVED] credits.

Sections: baseline; a 4-week pilot (scope, team); at least four
metrics with targets; training and support; governance (credit
caps, low-res-first rule, approval gates, the compliance skill,
AI labels, who may publish); the decision point to scale,
adjust or stop.

Check your work

  • Your capstone project holds the brief, the approved storyboard and the approved script.
  • References saved as Elements; every shot drafted low-res and the keepers upgraded within budget.
  • EP02_v1.mp4 exported: your film, 9:16, captions on, AI label on the end card.
  • The compliance review shows no high-risk findings.
  • A screenshot shows the private or draft upload with the AI label switched on.
  • The adoption plan has a baseline, a pilot, four metrics with targets, governance and a decision point.
