# Harbour Bean Co. — Synthetic Campaign Brief

This fictional brief exists only for C436 learning activities.

## Campaign

- Run ID: `HB-001`
- Audience: busy Singapore office workers who make coffee in a shared pantry
- Viewer problem: pantry coffee often tastes bitter and inconsistent
- Desired outcome: viewers can remember and try three controllable fixes
- Channel: short-form vertical video
- Target duration: 30 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Tone: warm, practical, calm, never judgmental
- Viewer action: save the three-fix checklist for the next cup

## Approved facts

1. Use a consistent coffee-to-water ratio.
2. Water that is too hot can contribute to unwanted bitterness.
3. Extending contact time can increase extraction and may intensify bitterness.
4. Changing one variable at a time makes it easier to identify the cause.

The campaign must not make health, medical, performance, or guaranteed taste claims.

## Brand system

- Primary: deep teal `#0F5C5E`
- Accent: warm amber `#F2A23A`
- Neutral: soft cream `#F7F1E3`
- Visual character: clean office pantry, natural light, measured tools, no visible real-world logos
- On-screen text: concise, high contrast, inside the central safe area

## Required controls

- Use only fictional people, hands, products, locations, and account identifiers.
- Do not imitate a living creator, employee, or public figure.
- Record the source, prompt, tool, date, rights note, and status of every asset.
- Keep public posting disabled during the course.
- A person must approve claims, rights, the final edit, disclosure, and release scope.
- Use supplied placeholder media if a live generator is unavailable or unapproved.
