# Hands On Labs for Agentic AI for Video Creation

These eight Hermes-native labs form one connected journey from installation and MiniMax M3 configuration to prompt engineering, video tools and skills, custom brand production, multi-agent Kanban review, private YouTube upload and controlled scheduling.

| # | Lab | Alignment | Primary output |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Set Up Hermes Desktop and Connect MiniMax M3](lab-01-setup-hermes-and-connect-minimax-m3/README.md) | Topic 1 | `setup-evidence.json and redacted diagnostic screenshot` |
| 2 | [Prompt Hermes to Create a Simple Video](lab-02-prompt-hermes-to-create-a-simple-video/README.md) | Topic 1 | `simple-video.mp4, shot-plan.json and ffprobe.json` |
| 3 | [Engineer Video Prompts with FRAME-CUT](lab-03-engineer-video-prompts-with-frame-cut/README.md) | Topic 1 | `prompt-pack.json and prompt-score.csv` |
| 4 | [Install Video Tools and Hermes Skills](lab-04-install-video-tools-and-skills/README.md) | Topic 2 | `tool-routing.json and skill-smoke-test.json` |
| 5 | [Create a Custom Branded Video Skill](lab-05-create-custom-branded-video-skill/README.md) | Topic 2 | `custom-video.mp4, brand-review.json and render-evidence.json` |
| 6 | [Build the Multi-Agent Video Workflow](lab-06-build-multi-agent-video-workflow/README.md) | Topic 3 | `multi-agent-plan.json and four verified handoff records` |
| 7 | [Orchestrate Kanban Review and YouTube Upload](lab-07-orchestrate-kanban-review-and-youtube-upload/README.md) | Topic 3 | `kanban-export.json, approval-ledger.json and private-upload receipt or dry-run preview` |
| 8 | [Schedule Controlled Video Publishing with Hermes Cron](lab-08-schedule-controlled-video-publishing/README.md) | Topic 3 | `cron-preview.json and operations-ledger.csv` |

## Connected workflow

`Hermes setup -> MiniMax M3 -> FRAME-CUT prompt -> tool and skill routing -> custom branded video -> specialist agents -> Kanban review and private upload -> paused cron release`
