# Hands-On Labs for Agentic AI for Video Creation

These four connected labs take one video from a simple first draft to a polished tool-assisted version, a multi-agent team workflow, and a consistent one-minute micro-drama episode.

| # | Lab | Topic | Main result |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | [Create Your First Video with Hermes](lab-01-create-first-video-with-hermes/README.md) | Topic 1 | simple-video.mp4, first-video-prompt.md and review-notes.md |
| 2 | [Improve the Video with Tools and Skills](lab-02-improve-video-with-tools-and-skills/README.md) | Topic 2 | improved-video.mp4, tool-and-skill-plan.md and before-after-review.md |
| 3 | [Improve the Video with a Multi-Agent Team](lab-03-multi-agent-video-kanban-youtube-schedule/README.md) | Topic 3 | team-video.mp4, kanban-export.json, audit-review.md, private-upload-preview.json and schedule-preview.json |
| 4 | [Create a One-Minute Micro-Drama Episode](lab-04-create-one-minute-micro-drama-series/README.md) | Topic 4 | micro-drama-pilot.mp4, series-bible.yaml, character-bible.yaml, storyboard.md and continuity-review.md |
