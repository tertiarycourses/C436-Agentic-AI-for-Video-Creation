Lab 04 — Create Episode 1 with Claude and Higgsfield

Claude Desktop + Higgsfield MCP — the video-script and higgsfield-video skills · about 75 minutes

Direct Claude to produce the episode in Higgsfield — script, prompts, approval, budget, references, low-res drafts, then high-res finals — and keep every asset traceable in one Higgsfield folder.

Steps

  1. Script the shots — Prompt A calls the video-script skill: shots, VO, asset descriptions and two video prompts per shot, as an artifact.
  2. Add cinematic effects, approve — Prompt Builder (Stage Scene creation): tick camera presets, angles, lens, lighting, grade; paste as changes; reply APPROVED.
  3. Agree the budget — Prompt B calls the higgsfield-video skill: balance, models_explore, a credit table at draft and final resolution.
  4. References first — Claude calls generate_image for Rachel, Ah Ma and the tin. Pick one still each.
  5. Draft every shot low-res — Cheap model, 480p or 720p, generate_video_batch then jobs_wait. Fix the story here, where a re-roll is cheap.
  6. Upgrade the keepers — Prompt C re-makes only the approved drafts at 1080p on the final model, from the same still and prompt.
  7. File, download, hand off — Move every job into the Higgsfield folder; save each clip as S01-SH01.mp4 in 05-edit; download lahlah-edit.json and captions.srt.

Prompts

PROMPT A — shots, assets and video prompts (video-script skill)
Use the video-script skill on the approved storyboard.

For every shot give: shot ID, timeline seconds, the one action,
dialogue or VO, on-screen text (added in the editor, never
generated), the assets in shot with their five fixed traits,
and two prompts:
- i2v: animate from the approved reference still;
- t2v: fallback with no still.

Scene prompt formula:
[SHOT_SIZE] [ANGLE] of [SUBJECT], [ACTION], in
[LOCATION_TIME], [LIGHTING], [MOOD], [COLOUR_GRADE],
camera: [CAMERA_MOVE], [LENS], 9:16, no on-screen text,
no logos.

Show it as an HTML artifact marked DRAFT and stop.
PROMPT B — budget, references, low-res drafts (higgsfield-video skill)
Use the higgsfield-video skill to produce Episode 1.

1. Check the storyboard and script are APPROVED.
2. Call balance and models_explore: a cheap draft model
   (480p or 720p) and a final model (1080p).
3. Credit table: stills, low-res drafts, 1080p upgrades,
   +30%. Budget [BUDGET] credits. Ask before every batch.
4. On Go: generate_image for Rachel, Ah Ma and the tin;
   wait for my picks.
5. Draft every shot LOW-RES with generate_video_batch;
   poll jobs_wait; keep the status table. No 1080p yet.
6. Folder: "Kopi & Coins – Ep1 micro-drama (TikTok)".
   List the job IDs to Move into it.
PROMPT C — upgrade the keepers to high resolution
These drafts are approved: [SHOT_IDS]. Re-generate ONLY these at
1080p on the final model, from the same reference still, the
same prompt and the same camera preset. Show the cost first.
Leave rejected drafts alone. Add the new job IDs to the status
table and the folder list.
PROMPT D — re-roll one draft, then hand off
S03-SH01 drifted: the tin has no dent and the labels are
garbled. Re-roll this LOW-RES draft only, from the approved tin
still, camera Static, no text in the prompt — we add the
labels in LahLah Video.

When every shot is final, write lahlah-edit.json (schema
lahlah-edit/1, platform tiktok, 9:16, 30 fps, clips in timeline
order with trim_in and trim_out, transitions from the
storyboard, captions in timeline seconds) and captions.srt.
Give me the 1080p download links in order, named S01-SH01.mp4
and so on, and the total credits spent.
